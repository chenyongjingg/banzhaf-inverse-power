# Appendix: explicit closed-form solutions (G, F) for all 6 ≤ n ≤ 80

For each n, the table gives the W-family parameters (G, F) with winning coalitions
v(G,F)(S)=1 iff |S|≥n−1, or (|S|=n−2 and N\S∈G), or (|S|=n−3 and N\S∈F).
Every entry is verified to have Bz(v)=ψⁿ (swings proportional to (2,…,2,1)) and to be
monotone (Pairs(F) ⊆ G). Data in machine-readable form: appendix-data-n80.json. The summary
table below lists (n, c, |G|, |F|, method) for all 6 ≤ n ≤ 80; entries for 41 ≤ n ≤ 80 are
generated by the closed-form constructions of Propositions 5 and 11 (manuscript §11) and every
entry is re-verified by direct swing enumeration against ψⁿ and by the monotonicity check
Pairs(F) ⊆ G.
